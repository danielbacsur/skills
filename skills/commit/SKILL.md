---
name: commit
description: Use whenever committing changes, amending or rewording a commit, or suggesting a commit message.
---

Write the subject as a conventional commit, `<type>: <description>`, using one of `feat`, `fix`, `docs`, `style`, `refactor`, `perf`, `test`, `build`, `ci`, `chore` or `revert`, without a scope in parentheses. Keep the whole subject lowercase, including names, and between 40 and 60 characters long, aiming for about 50 and never going past 72, by choosing the wording rather than padding it out or cutting meaning. Credit yourself as a co-author with the `Co-Authored-By` trailer your harness provides, and keep it as given.
